About

Making people better.

Arthur Curtis is an independent, tooling-led levy and apprenticeship consultancy. We exist to make one of the most misunderstood budgets in British business actually work.

Why we exist

The levy is a good idea, badly used.

Every large employer in the country pays the apprenticeship levy. Most let a meaningful chunk of it expire — not through carelessness, but because the rules are dense and the advice on offer usually comes from someone with training to sell.

We started Arthur Curtis to fix that from the other side: independent, and led by tools instead of slides. If we tell you your levy funds fourteen apprentices, you can check it yourself in the same calculator we used. The recommendation and the proof live in the same place.

The name on the door is a promise about standards. The work behind it is software — and the discipline to only ever show you a number we'd stake our own name on.

What we believe

Trust is the whole product.

  • ✓Independence over incentive. We don't deliver training, so we've no reason to point you anywhere but the right answer.
  • ✓Show the working. Every figure traces back to gov.uk or a dated funding rulebook. If we can't source it, we don't say it. See how →
  • ✓Tools before talk. We'd rather hand you working software than a presentation about it.
  • ✓People, not headcount. The goal is careers and capability that outlast the funding — "making people better" is the brief, not a tagline.
How we work with you

Independent by design.

We sit on your side of the table. That means we plan the spend, engineer the funding, and prove the outcome — then help you choose and hold providers to account, rather than being one.

It's a deliberately smaller promise than "we'll do everything," and a more honest one. The result is a levy strategy your finance director and your HR director can both put their name to.
