Our story

Why we exist.

Every levy-paying employer contributes to developing the workforce. Most of it expires unspent — not for lack of will, but for lack of a clear, trustworthy way to turn funding into a plan.

Arthur Curtis closes that gap: real, working tools that show you exactly what your levy can build, and a development model — the 2-5 Model — that turns training into retention.
