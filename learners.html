The basics

What an apprenticeship actually is.

You earn while you learn

A paid job from day one. You’re an employee, not a student — with a wage, holiday and a team.

A real qualification

You train to a national standard and finish with a recognised qualification — the same one across every employer.

No tuition debt

The training is funded — through the apprenticeship levy employers pay, and government funding. You don’t pay for it.

Time set aside to learn

Part of every week is protected off-the-job training — so you’re building skills, not just doing the work.

Our Way · the 2-5 pathway

Where an apprenticeship can take you.

We don’t see an apprenticeship as a one-off course. It’s the first leg of a route from junior to senior — funded the whole way.

Start · Year 0
Junior
You join and begin training alongside real work.
Year 2
Competent
Trained to the standard and working independently.
Year 3
Applying
Embedding the skills, taking on more, mentoring newer starters.
Year 5
Senior
A further funded block takes you junior → senior.

Competent by year two, senior by year five — on around five years of real, on-the-job experience. It’s a career built by doing, and it’s how good employers grow their own seniors instead of hiring them in. More on Our Way →
