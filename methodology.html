How the levy number is built

Five steps, no black box.

The headline "your levy funds N apprentices" is one short calculation on your pay bill — the same steps HMRC and the ESFA use. Here it is in full:

pay bill × 0.5% − £15,000 allowance = your levy pot
levy pot + 10% government top-up = what you can spend
what you can spend ÷ the funding band = apprenticeships

The NI saving, co-investment, incentives and the expiry clock all run the same way — a published rate or threshold, applied plainly. Each of those numbers is in the rulebook below, with its source.

What we don't claim

The honest edges.

  • !Averages aren't rules. The ~£9,000 "average band" used for the headline apprentice count is indicative only — real funding bands run £1,500–£27,000 per standard.
  • !England, for now. These rules are the ESFA's for England. Scotland (SDS) and Wales differ — multi-nation support is on the roadmap.
  • !Not financial advice. We model published funding rules; we're not licensed financial advisors, and none of this is personalised investment advice.
  • !Guidance, not a guarantee. Figures are indicative — your actual funding depends on your real pay bill, ages, standards and start dates. That's what a conversation is for.
One source of truth

The site and the calculator read the same rulebook.

Most tools drift: a number gets updated in one place and forgotten in another. We fixed that at the root. Every funding constant lives in a single canonical file that both this website and our full Fundable calculator fetch at runtime — so a rule is changed once, and everything updates together.

It means the number you see in a two-minute calculator and the number in a full funded plan are computed from the identical rulebook. No drift, no surprises. That file is public, and it's linked above.
